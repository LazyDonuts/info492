About this dataset

The team supplied 1,370 synthetic RFIs modeled around East Link and Downtown Redmond Link. RFI text, dates, costs and outcomes are generated examples, not official Sound Transit records.

Information timing

The source Data Dictionary marks routing metadata, descriptions and contractor impact flags as available at submission. Status, response times, estimates, schedule effects and change-order results are retrospective. Its estimate description says “early,” but its availability flag is N; this dashboard follows N.

Cost allocation

RFI-linked amounts use RFI_CO_Link allocations. Whole change-order approved amounts are shown separately in details as shared CO totals, never attributed in full to each RFI. Missing estimates are not zero cost.

Ground truth

Outcome-derived impact scores, priority tiers and ranks are retained only in explicitly labeled source metadata for future evaluation. They are never used for ranking, search or risk badges here.
