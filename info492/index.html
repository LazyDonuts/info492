Our Team Thesis

The thesis for our team is that Critical RFIs can be delayed when all requests are treated with similar priority, and AI-assisted impact assessment could reduce cost by 10% or more by helping GCs identify RFIs with the highest cost risk. According to an article in 2013, which looked at about 1,362 projects with an average of 796 RFI with a minimum of 100 RFI each, it cost them on average around $1,089 per request and about $859,000 total for the firms. That’s just looking at the ones that were reviewed and responded to. It’s not looking at the ones that got no reply, which was about 25% in the Americas (Hughes, et al., 2013). The amount of money can really eat away from the profit margin of an already low margin industry, and that’s just talking about the cost of processing them alone. The average turnaround time for a RFI in the US in 2018 was around 14 days, which is quite a bit of time (Aibinu et al., 2018). That amount of time could delay the schedule for the construction a lot, which would push the cost up by a lot, not to mention the missed deadlines. An AI-assisted impact assessment would be able to find the RFIs that would cause the highest cost risk so that we get to the most important RFIs fast.
