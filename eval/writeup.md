### Test Results and Future Considerations
During this demo, our team developed an AI agent that assigns response priorities to RFI based on cost risk and urgency. GCs will be enabled to find the most important RFIs quicker, leading to a reduction of time needed to sort through them. For this demo, counted accuracy, considering 70% accuracy or 7/10 RFIs ranked accurately. Our test results showed a 30% accuracy rate for both agents, with a simple-way score of 70% accuracy. Our failure groups are the two first tests that produced 30% accuracy scores. There were no counts of our AI Agent crossing lines and continuing to do a function that it was not made to do. In conclusion, our AI Agent did not work as expected, as it continuously failed to accurately categorize at least 7 out of 10 RFIs that were inputted. We would try loosening the criteria for categorization next time, as our simple model which categorized purely based on estimated cost as opposed to explicit mention of factors such as schedule impact for example, performed better.
